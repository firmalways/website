How FirmAlways Gate works

Gate sits between your employees' apps and your AI provider. It works with any tool that speaks the OpenAI API: change only base_url.

  1. 1

    An employee types or attaches a file

    In the apps they already use, the browser extension or the API.

  2. 2

    Gate inspects Thai data

    On your machine or in your cloud. The text goes nowhere else.

  3. 3

    Block

    Passwords, API keys or section 26 data about a person: nothing is sent.

    3

    Mask

    Real values become «PERSON_1», then the prompt goes to the AI.

  4. 4

    The AI replies

    Gate restores the real values and masks new personal data the AI may produce.

  5. 5

    A complete answer

    The audit log records only counts and data types, never the text.

What happens when the data reaches fraudsters

Text pasted into a personal AI chat may be stored outside your organisation. If it leaks through a hacked account, the provider's systems or a shared conversation link, fraudsters no longer have to guess: they have real details to sound credible.

1Posing as a child or relative

  • Full name
  • Address
  • Phone

A fraudster calls a customer's parents, uses the real name and the correct address, and says their child had an accident and needs money for treatment now. The real details leave no time for doubt.

The family transfers money that is rarely recovered.

2Call-centre gangs posing as officials

  • National ID
  • Birth date
  • ID card address

The caller claims to be police or a state officer, reads out the victim's ID number and birth date to "verify" them, then says their account is linked to money laundering and must be moved for "inspection".

Victims believe it because the caller knows what only the state should know.

3Opening accounts or loans in someone's name

  • National ID
  • Birth date
  • Nationality

ID card details pass weak identity checks to open mule accounts, sign up for services or apply for loans in the owner's name.

Debts and court cases land on a person who knew nothing.

4Blackmail and discrimination with sensitive data

  • Health results
  • Blood type
  • Religion

Health records, chronic conditions or criminal history are used for extortion, or to deny someone a job or insurance.

Harm to lives and reputations that cannot be undone.

Why organisations must care: the law requires it, and the harm is real

If one employee pastes customer data into an AI chat and it leaks, liability does not stop with the employee. The company, as the data controller, is liable too: civil, criminal and administrative.

Sensitive data under PDPA section 26

Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) requires explicit consent before collecting, using or disclosing these categories, unless a legal exception applies. ID card details such as address, birth date and nationality are personal data that make impersonation easy, so Gate masks them too.

What the law requires of organisations

Section 37(1)

Provide appropriate security measures to prevent loss, unauthorised access, use, alteration or disclosure of personal data.

Section 37(4)

Report a personal data breach to the PDPC Office within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and inform the data subjects when the risk is high.

Section 40

Data processors, such as AI providers, must keep data secure and report incidents to the controller, so you must know what you sent to whom.

Penalties when data leaks

TypeSectionPenalty
Civil77, 78Compensation for actual damage even without intent or negligence, plus punitive damages of up to twice the actual damage.
Criminal79Unlawful use or disclosure of sensitive data: up to 6 months' imprisonment or a fine of up to THB 500,000. For unlawful gain: up to 1 year or THB 1,000,000.
Executives81Directors or managers whose orders or omissions caused the company's offence are punished too.
Administrative82–84Fines of up to THB 1, 3 and 5 million by type of breach, including missing security measures or breach notification (section 83) and violations involving sensitive data (section 84).

These penalties are already enforced: in 2024 the PDPC Office announced a THB 7 million fine against a private company after leaked customer data was used in scams.

If an employee leaks customer data, what happens to the company

  1. The employee is held responsible: disciplinary action, and possible prosecution for unlawful disclosure.
  2. The company must notify the PDPC Office within 72 hours and say what data, whose, went where. Without that, the risk cannot even be assessed.
  3. Customers sue for damages: the company is liable even without intent, and may pay punitive damages on top.
  4. Investigation and fines: no security measures, no backups, no record of processing or no breach notification can mean administrative fines of several million baht.
  5. Trust is lost: customers and partners walk away, which often costs more than the fine.

Related international standards

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Information security management. Controls A.8.11 Data masking and A.8.12 Data leakage prevention match what Gate does, along with A.5.34 Privacy and protection of PII.

ISO/IEC 27701:2019

The privacy extension to ISO 27001, covering personal data management as a controller and as a processor.

ISO/IEC 42001:2023

AI management systems: control the data used with AI and assess the risks to individuals.

Install FirmAlways Gate

Requires Python 3.11 or newer, or Docker.

Install script

curl -fsSL https://firmalways.meapplication.co.th/install.sh | sh

The script installs the latest release with uv, pipx or pip. Read it before running it, or use the same script from the GitHub release.

curl -fsSL https://github.com/firmalways/gate/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

Docker

docker run --rm -p 8787:8787 -e FIRMALWAYS_GATE_API_KEY=change-me \
  -v "$PWD/policy.toml:/etc/firmalways/policy.toml:ro" ghcr.io/firmalways/gate

See what an AI would receive

firmalways-gate scan "ลูกค้าคุณสมชาย ใจดี เลขบัตร 1101700203611"

Use it with your existing apps

from openai import OpenAI
client = OpenAI(base_url="http://127.0.0.1:8787/v1", api_key="<FIRMALWAYS_GATE_API_KEY>")

Works with OpenAI, Azure OpenAI in your own subscription, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint and local models such as Ollama. See every option in the README, and the browser extension for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini in extension.
