What FirmAlways Personal does for you

Protection every time you use AI

Checks on paste and before send

Pasted text is checked before it enters the box; typed text is checked when you press Enter or Send.

Mask, then restore

Thai ID numbers (checksum-verified), phones, email, accounts, cards, titled names, addresses and birth dates become placeholders, and come back in the answer for you only.

Stops what should never be sent

Passwords, API keys and PDPA section 26 sensitive data about a person stay on your device.

Privacy Mirror

See your text next to what the AI actually received, or have it ask you before every send.

Fully adjustable, all free

Basic and Advanced mode

Four switches for most people, or an action per data type: allow, warn, ask, mask, redact, block.

Your keywords and rules

Add project names, key customers or employee ID patterns you want to protect.

Policy library

Thailand PDPA Baseline, Global Privacy Baseline, Personal Finance Protection (OTP, CVV, PIN) and Developer Secret Protection, each with its legal and ISO references.

Privacy report

See what you nearly sent to an AI over 7 or 30 days. Counts and types only, never text.

It also has a scan page for text and .txt .md .csv .json .docx files, and a one-page brief to recommend FirmAlways to your manager or DPO, with none of your usage data.

Install in one minute

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers. Until it is in the Chrome Web Store, install it from the file:

  1. Download firmalways-personal-v0.1.0.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and you are protected.

All the code is on GitHub under Apache-2.0.

Your text goes nowhere

  • Checked in your browser. The extension sends no text or usage data to FirmAlways or anyone else.
  • Masked real values live in memory and are gone when the browser closes or you press "Forget values".
  • The report keeps counts, data types and site names, never text.
  • Free for good, no message limits, and detection is never weakened to make you upgrade.

Limits you should know

  • Typed text sits in the site's box before you send, so the site can technically read it. Pasting is safer because it is checked first.
  • Uploaded files and images, voice and mobile apps are not checked yet. Use the scan page to make a safe copy first.
  • Names without a title, and data that identifies someone only through context, may be missed.
  • Placeholder-masked data is still personal data under the PDPA. This tool reduces risk; it does not make you compliant by itself.
