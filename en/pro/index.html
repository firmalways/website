FirmAlways Pro lets each department use AI with the data it is responsible for

Gate for organisations. HR may send employee names, Sales may not send customer phone numbers, every department is checked for section 26 sensitive data, and your DPO gets a Thai-language report ready for the ROPA.

What FirmAlways Pro adds to Gate

For IT and the DPO

Per-department policies

Each app or team has its own key. Decide which data types it may send, mask or block, which models it may use, and what to redact in answers.

Report for the DPO

Which AI provider received which kinds of data, masked or as is, what was blocked and why, per department, in Thai.

For installation and operation

Offline licence

Signature checked on your machine. No internet needed, and no usage data sent back to us.

Installation help

We set up policies, connect the AI providers you use, and roll out the browser extension to employees.

Every plan includes all of Gate's features: Thai data masking, section 26 blocking, answer scanning and file inspection.

Your data stays in your systems

Your cloud

Deploy with Docker on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud or a Thai cloud you already use, connected to your own AI account such as Azure OpenAI or AWS Bedrock.

Your office

Install on an internal server. Works without internet when requests go to a local model.

  • We have no access to your systems, prompts or audit log.
  • Pro never contacts our servers, neither to check the licence nor while running.
